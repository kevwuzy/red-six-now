ABOUT US

Refusing formulas, crafting your signature aesthetic.

Based in Singapore, RED SIX NOW is a premier studio and academy dedicated to high-end styling and professional makeup education. We believe that true beauty is never a standardized template, but the precise refinement of individual identity.

Our specialty is 1-on-1 daily and event styling for everyday individuals. Whether it is a date night, an important interview, a celebration, or simply a desire to shine in daily life, we provide personalized private sessions that honor your unique features — alongside bridal styling, executive personal branding, and professional education.

At RED SIX NOW, beauty is not a privilege for the few — it is for everyone looking to discover their most luminous self.
